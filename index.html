what is internship


Internships are two types

Online Internship & Offline Internships

online internship - An online internship is a short-term work experience where students or beginners learn real job and skills by working with a company or organization.


Offline internship- An internship where you physically go to company, office, or workplace to dp work and training


in an offline internship:

. you work from the office

. Meet team members directly

.Attend meetings in person

.Learn workplace environment and discipline

Use company's computer and resources

Internship are important because they helps students gain real-work experience and practical skills before starting a full time job.


Benefits of internship

. Improve skills

. Gain work experience

. Learn team work and communication skills

. Increase job opportunities
